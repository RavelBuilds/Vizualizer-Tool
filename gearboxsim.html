Load your PlantSim HTML report to begin

1. Drop Data

Export your simulation from Plant Simulation as an HTML report (.htm). Drag and drop the file directly onto this screen to instantly generate the topology map without uploading to any server.

2. Explore Map

Pan and zoom around the interactive D3.js flow map. The system automatically connects machines and assigns roles based on real-time utilization. Hover over any machine or buffer to see instant statistics.

3. Fix Bottlenecks

The Visualizer identifies Bottlenecks (red) and Constrained stations (amber — either starved or blocked). Click the "Fix" link in the Constraints panel for actionable, PlantSim-specific recommendations.
